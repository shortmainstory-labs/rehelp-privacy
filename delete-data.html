Data Deletion Request

Delete Your Data

ReHelp respects your right to privacy and data protection. You can request deletion of all your data at any time.

⚠️ Important: ReHelp uses an "offline-first" architecture. All your data is stored LOCALLY on your device, not on our servers. This means you already have full control over your data!

What data does ReHelp store?

Locally on your phone:

  • Your ReHelp ID
  • Your Circle contacts list
  • Medical card information (if added)
  • SOS alert history
  • App settings and preferences

NOT stored on our servers:

  • No cloud backups
  • No tracking or analytics
  • No personal information on external servers

How to delete your data:

Method 1: Uninstall the app (Easiest)

  1. Open your Android Settings
  2. Go to Apps → ReHelp
  3. Tap "Uninstall"
  4. ✅ All your data is automatically deleted from your device!

Method 2: Clear app data

  1. Android Settings → Apps → ReHelp
  2. Storage → Clear Data
  3. ✅ All data deleted (app remains installed)

Method 3: Email deletion request

If you want written confirmation of data deletion or have questions, you can contact us via email:

Submit Deletion Request

Click the button below to send us an email request:

📧 Send Deletion Request

We will confirm deletion within 48 hours

What happens after deletion?

  • ✅ All your local data is permanently deleted
  • ✅ Your ReHelp ID becomes inactive
  • ✅ Circle contacts will no longer receive alerts from you
  • ✅ SOS alert history is removed
  • ✅ Medical card information is deleted

Processing Time

Local deletion (Methods 1 & 2): Immediate

Email confirmation (Method 3): Within 48 hours
